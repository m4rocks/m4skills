Prove it on the real artifact after the last edit, sized to the change: a typo needs only the existing checks passing, a behavior needs a check that shows it. For UI, take a screenshot, open it and compare it with what you expected; without computer-use tools, drive the app from the shell with OS automation. List what stayed unverified.
