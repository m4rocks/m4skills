---
name: verify
description: Use when the user says verify, prove it, check it works or test it manually.
---

Prove it on the real artifact after the last edit, sized to the change: a typo needs only the existing checks passing, a behavior needs a check that shows it. Run the narrowest check while iterating and the full suite once at the end. For UI, screenshot only the changed view, cropped to its window, open it and compare it with what you expected; without computer-use tools, drive the app from the shell with OS automation. List what stayed unverified.
