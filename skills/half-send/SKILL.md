name: half-send
description: Only on 'half send', 'full stop', 'let me review', 'don't merge'.
---

Run `full-send`, but stop with the PR ready to merge. Never merge, enable auto-merge, enter a merge queue or push to the default branch; turn off auto-merge or a queue already on, and check again before your last push. End with the gate's verdict: ready, waiting only on a required human approval, or blocked with what blocks it and what you tried.
